Our Philosophy

Crafted for Quiet Mornings & Unhurried Afternoons

"We believe good coffee is never rushed. It's an invitation to pause, meet a collaborator, read another chapter, or simply savor five quiet minutes before the day speeds up."

Founded in 2024, Ochre Coffee Roasters began with an uncomplicated goal: build an honest space celebrating the craft of Indian specialty coffee. We partner directly with sustainable coffee estates in Karnataka and Araku, roasting exclusively on a fluid-air bed to coax out gentle florals, sweet honey, and chocolate undertones without bitter scorching.
