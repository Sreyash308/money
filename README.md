# Ochre Coffee Roasters - Specialty Coffee & Slow Living

A modern, production-grade specialty coffee roastery web application crafted with semantic HTML5, modern Vanilla CSS, and lightweight Vanilla JavaScript.

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://money-iota-woad.vercel.app)

**Live Production**: [https://money-iota-woad.vercel.app](https://money-iota-woad.vercel.app)

---

## Brand & Design Direction

- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist European & Japanese specialty café modernism.
- **Palette**: Warm off-white (`#fbfaf8`), espresso & charcoal (`#1e1b17`), terracotta & ochre (`#b85d39`), and natural sage (`#5e6653`).
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (`Plus Jakarta Sans` for high-contrast geometric headings & body, `Caveat` for warm hand-drawn accents).
- **Zero Heavy Dependencies**: Pure native web standards, optimized for performance, accessibility, and smooth 60fps micro-interactions.

---

## Key Features

1. **Sticky Header & Mobile Drawer Navigation**: Slim frosted-glass navbar with backdrop blur, scroll spy, and full-screen accessible mobile navigation drawer.
2. **Editorial Hero Showcase**: High-impact typography, live roastery opening hours pill, dual CTAs, and featured ceramic flat white badge.
3. **Café Favorites / Must-Try Spotlight**: 4 signature highlighted drinks and food items with high-res photography, custom badges, and flavor notes.
4. **Interactive Categorized Menu**:
   - Filterable tabs: *All Items*, *Coffee & Brews*, *Coolers & Iced*, *Tea & Infusions*, *Kitchen & Bites*, *Bakery & Sweet*.
   - Instant dietary toggles: *Vegetarian Only* and *Chilled Only*.
   - Item descriptions, Indian Rupee (`₹`) pricing, origin tags, and dietary indicators.
5. **Brand Story & Philosophy**: Authentic human storytelling on mindful slow living, fluid-air bed roasting, and direct estate trade.
6. **Café Experience Grid**: Specialty pour-overs, dedicated workspace with fiber Wi-Fi, artisan kitchen, and sunlit atmosphere.
7. **Asymmetric Editorial Gallery**: Curated photo grid capturing barista craftsmanship, morning pastries, whole beans, and roastery seating.
8. **Visit Us & Location Section**: Full opening hours table, address in Indiranagar Bengaluru, direct phone & Instagram links, and stylized interactive map card.
9. **Production Footer**: Brand summary, quick navigation links, opening hours, and roast dispatch newsletter form.
10. **SEO & Structured Data**: Built-in Schema.org `CafeOrCoffeeShop` JSON-LD markup, OpenGraph preview cards, Twitter cards, and semantic headings.

## Local Development

Run with any local HTTP server:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Sreyash308/money.git
cd money

# Start static server
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
