Merchant Production Activation Checklist

  1. Razorpay Account Setup: Sign up at Razorpay Dashboard and complete merchant business KYC.
  2. Link Destination Account: In Razorpay Dashboard → Settings → Bank Account & Settlements, link your primary bank account and verify UPI settlement to 9182916879@ybl.
  3. Obtain Live API Keys: Generate live RAZORPAY_KEY_ID and RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET from API Keys.
  4. Configure Webhook: In Razorpay Dashboard → Webhooks, add your Vercel endpoint: https://your-domain.vercel.app/api/payments/razorpay/webhook with secret RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET, listening for payment.captured and order.paid.
